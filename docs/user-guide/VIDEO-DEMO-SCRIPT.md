# Shiftly Demo Video — Recording Script

## Recommended format

- **Primary video:** “Create and Publish a Weekly Schedule in Shiftly”
- **Length:** 5–7 minutes
- **Resolution:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps
- **Style:** Screen recording with a calm voice-over, visible pointer, and short zooms on important status messages
- **Sample data:** Use fictional employees and a non-production demonstration account
- **Editing:** Remove loading time, mistakes, passwords, tokens, email addresses, and notification pop-ups

## Before recording

1. Prepare four fictional employees in at least three positions.
2. Add recurring availability for each employee.
3. Add one pending time-off request.
4. Configure one staffing requirement that initially creates a coverage gap.
5. Prepare a week containing both published shifts and three draft shifts.
6. Set the browser zoom to 100% and hide bookmarks and personal extensions.
7. Turn on **Do Not Disturb** and close unrelated tabs.
8. Rehearse once and confirm that publishing in the demo environment is safe.

## Scene-by-scene script

| Time | Screen action | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Show title card, then the sign-in screen. | “In this video, you’ll create, check, and publish a weekly employee schedule in Shiftly.” |
| 0:15–0:35 | Sign in as a Supervisor. Pause on the Schedule page. | “Managers land on the weekly schedule, where coverage, conflicts, and forecast labour cost are visible at a glance.” |
| 0:35–1:05 | Open **Employees** and select one employee. Briefly show position, hours, skills, and status. | “Start by confirming that employee details, work limits, and skills are current.” |
| 1:05–1:35 | Open **Availability**. Add or show a Monday morning range and save. | “Availability records the employee’s recurring weekly pattern. Specific leave dates belong in Time off.” |
| 1:35–2:05 | Open **Time off**. Review the pending request and approve it. | “Review pending leave before scheduling the affected week so Shiftly can prevent invalid assignments.” |
| 2:05–2:40 | Return to **Schedule**. Point to coverage, gaps, conflicts, cost, and the draft notice. | “The week-at-a-glance panel highlights operational risk. Drafts remain hidden from employees until publication.” |
| 2:40–3:25 | Select **+ Create shift**. Fill employee, date, times, role, break, and required skill. Save. | “Create the shift, review eligibility feedback, and save it as a draft.” |
| 3:25–4:00 | Select two shift checkboxes. Demonstrate a bulk edit, then use Undo. | “Checkboxes enable fast bulk edits and duplication. Undo is available immediately after a schedule change.” |
| 4:00–4:25 | Open **Coverage view** and point to the staffing gap. Add/reassign a shift so the gap is resolved. | “Coverage view compares scheduled staffing with requirements hour by hour.” |
| 4:25–4:50 | Open **Reports** and point to coverage, overtime, and labour-cost forecast. | “Before publishing, check coverage and overtime cost in Reports.” |
| 4:50–5:25 | Return to **Schedule**. Show zero conflicts, then select **Publish week**. | “When readiness checks pass, publish the week. Employees can now see their assigned shifts.” |
| 5:25–5:50 | Sign in as an Employee or cut to an employee session. Show **My schedule**. | “Employees see only published shifts and can manage availability, time off, swaps, and notification preferences.” |
| 5:50–6:05 | End card with support contact or internal help link. | “That’s the complete schedule workflow—from employee setup through publication.” |

## Recording sequence

1. Record the manager workflow in one take without narration.
2. Record the employee view as a separate clip.
3. Edit out waits and accidental pointer movement.
4. Add the voice-over using the narration column.
5. Add a brief on-screen label whenever the page changes.
6. Add captions and verify names, dates, and button labels.
7. Blur any real email address or organization information.
8. Export as H.264 MP4 at 1080p.
9. Watch the exported file once with sound and once muted to confirm captions are sufficient.

## Optional 90-second employee video

1. Sign in and open **My schedule**.
2. Open a published shift and review its details.
3. Add a recurring availability range.
4. Submit a time-off request.
5. Show where to request a shift swap.
6. Enable schedule notifications.
7. End with: “Only published shifts appear in My schedule.”

## Accessibility and quality checklist

- Captions are accurate and synchronized.
- Pointer movement is slow enough to follow.
- Important controls remain on screen for at least two seconds.
- Text is readable at normal playback size.
- Audio is clear and consistent, without background music under key instructions.
- The recording contains no real employee data or credentials.
- The final video is tested on both desktop and mobile playback.
